Status rule: red if 7-day average transits are under 50% of the same week a year earlier, or a headline from the last 7 days names the place and reports an attack on shipping; amber if under 85% of a year earlier, or such a headline reports a warning, diversion or suspension; otherwise green. Headline flags are set automatically by keyword and shown in the panel so you can judge them.
